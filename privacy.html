Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy & NDPA Compliance

How TOMITECH Compware Services Limited protects your facility blueprints, contact data, and corporate information in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

1. Commitment to Data Privacy

TOMITECH Compware Services Limited ("TOMITECH", "we", "our") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data protection and cybersecurity. As an ICT contractor handling sensitive security infrastructure designs and premises blueprints, confidentiality is integral to our operations.

2. Compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)

All personal information submitted through our contact forms, quote requests, or during site surveys is processed in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 under the regulatory oversight of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

3. What Data We Collect

  • Contact Identification: Full Name, corporate email address, and direct phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Facility Parameters: Project address, facility type (e.g. market, corporate office, estate), and scope requirements.
  • Engineering Documentation: Architectural floor plans, CAD drawings, or Bill of Quantities (BOQ) voluntarily uploaded for survey preparation.

4. How Data Is Protected & Used

Uploaded architectural drawings and floor plans are stored on encrypted internal arrays with restricted engineer access. We never sell, lease, or share client information with third-party advertising brokers. Data is used exclusively to prepare quotations, dispatch surveyors, and maintain service level agreements.
