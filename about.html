Our Story

Over 15 Years of Proven Engineering Delivery in Lagos

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, TOMITECH Compware Services Limited specializes in high-density IP surveillance systems, enterprise video management software, biometric turnstiles, and Cisco-certified structured network infrastructure.

We have grown by solving Nigeria's toughest infrastructure challenges: heat, extreme humidity, dirty electrical power grids, generator spikes, and dense commercial traffic. Where consumer-grade installers fail, our installations thrive.

From engineering the entire 151-camera IP surveillance grid at the New Tejuosho Market to safeguarding multi-tenant corporate headquarters in Victoria Island, our systems are built to run 24/7/365 without signal dropouts.
