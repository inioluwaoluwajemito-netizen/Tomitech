Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Engineering & Procurement Questions

Everything you need to know about our implementation processes, equipment guarantees, and warranties.

Do you provide a formal site survey before submitting an estimate? ▼

Yes. For all commercial facilities, markets, and multi-tenant complexes in Lagos, our systems engineers perform an in-person site survey. We evaluate cable paths, power availability, optical distances, and deliver an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with zero obligation.

Can your VMS integrate with our existing cameras or do we have to replace everything? ▼

We work with open-standard ONVIF compliant systems (such as Milestone and Nx Witness). In most cases, your existing IP cameras can be integrated directly into the new central VMS, allowing you to upgrade legacy cameras in phases without throwing away working hardware.

How do you handle power fluctuations and surges in Nigeria? ▼

All TOMITECH deployments include industrial-grade surge suppression, isolated earth ground bonding, and high-efficiency online double-conversion UPS batteries to ensure cameras and switches survive generator switchovers and utility spikes without equipment damage.

What warranties do you provide on installed equipment? ▼

All hardware sourced through TOMITECH carries manufacturer warranties (typically 2 to 3 years for enterprise IP cameras and Cisco hardware). Additionally, we provide a 12-month comprehensive workmanship warranty on all cabling, termination, and mounting.

Can we monitor cameras securely from mobile devices or overseas? ▼

Yes. We implement secure remote access using encrypted Sophos VPN tunnels or authenticated HTTPS zero-trust relays, avoiding risky open port forwarding so directors can securely view live feeds on iOS, Android, and laptops from anywhere in the world.
