Flagship Enterprise Project

New Tejuosho Ultra-Modern Market, Yaba, Lagos

151-Camera High-Density IP Surveillance & Centralized Control Room

Scale

151 IP Cameras

01. Client & Facility Profile

Client: Market Concession Management & Retail Facility Directorate.
Facility: 4-story retail commercial plaza with over 1,500 active shops, banking branches, open food courts, and underground vehicle parking bays in central Yaba, Lagos.

02. The Challenge

Severe shoplifting at peak market hours, unmonitored stairwell blind spots, and frequent generator switchover electrical surges that previously destroyed budget DVRs. The facility needed uninterrupted recording without blind spots across 4 separate floors.

03. The TOMITECH Solution

Installed 151 IK10 vandal-resistant dome cameras with starlight optical sensors. Designed a redundant 1Gbps fiber optic ring backbone interconnecting 8 floor distribution switches with isolated CCTV VLANs and a 24/7 central video wall with Milestone VMS.

04. The Measurable Result

82% reduction in recorded retail thefts within 90 days. Zero camera downtime during generator switchovers thanks to centralized online UPS protection, and forensic evidence that successfully assisted state police in 14 criminal recoveries.

Technologies Deployed at Tejuosho:

Milestone Systems VMS • 151x IK10 Dome Cameras • Cisco Catalyst Switches • 60-Day RAID 6 Storage Array

Enterprise Access & Cyber

Victoria Island Corporate Tower, Lagos

Biometric Speed Turnstiles, Tailgating Defense & Sophos Network Security

Throughput

2,200 Persons/Day

01. Client & Facility Profile

Client: Multi-tenant Grade-A Corporate Tower housing multinational oil & gas and financial institutions on Victoria Island, Lagos.

02. The Challenge

Heavy morning lobby bottlenecks, unauthorized delivery personnel wandering tenant floors, and frequent phishing attacks targeting shared internet uplinks across tenant subnets.

03. The TOMITECH Solution

Installed 6 motorized optical glass speed gates with 0.2s facial recognition and HID proximity readers. Integrated Sophos XGS 3300 next-gen firewalls with strict tenant VLAN segmentation and automated anti-tailgating alarms.

04. The Measurable Result

Lobby wait time dropped from 14 minutes down to 90 seconds during peak morning hours. 100% visitor compliance achieved, and zero security breaches across 3 consecutive audit cycles.

Perimeter Surveillance & Fiber Backbone

Lekki Peninsula Waterfront Gated Community

Perimeter Thermal CCTV, ANPR Plate Recognition & Fiber Optic Ring

Perimeter

3.8 Kilometers

01. Client & Facility Profile

Client: Residential Landlords Association & Estate Security Committee.
Facility: High-end 45-hectare residential community bordering the Lekki Lagoon with 320 luxury villas.

02. The Challenge

Vulnerable waterfront border where small boats landed undetected at night. Coastal humidity and violent lightning storms constantly blew out ordinary outdoor copper ethernet cables.

03. The TOMITECH Solution

Deployed a 3.8km armored fiber-optic ring immune to electrical lightning strikes. Positioned long-range thermal PTZ cameras with automated radar line-crossing alarms along the lagoon, plus dual ANPR cameras at the main vehicular estate gates.

04. The Measurable Result

100% perimeter thermal visibility with instant audio-visual alarms alerting the guard post within 1.5 seconds of waterline breach. Zero equipment failures across 3 heavy coastal rainy seasons.
